The Plastic Recycling Challenge

A recycling center received a mix of five plastics. Use liquids of different densities to separate every plastic into its own tray.

Remember: A solid floats if it is less dense than the liquid. It sinks if it is more dense than the liquid.

How to use

  1. Pick a tray of plastics.
  2. Pick a liquid from the shelf.
  3. Press Pour in and watch what floats and what sinks.
  4. Collect the floaters and sinkers into new trays. Repeat until every tray has only one kind of plastic.
